Operating model

Product intent, delivery and telemetry form one loop

Agents perform bounded, reviewable work. Product, engineering and service owners retain decision accountability.

01 · AI PDLC

Idea to approved direction

Agents ground ideas in strategy, customer context, architecture and prior decisions, then prepare product briefs, Jira decomposition and design inputs.

Human control: product and design owners approve value, scope and acceptance criteria.

02 · AI SDLC

Story to reviewable pull request

Agents use repository context, standards and dependencies to plan changes, generate code and tests, and run quality and security tools.

Human control: engineers approve architecture, test evidence and production risk.

03 · TDLC

Telemetry to validated improvement

Agents connect traces, SLOs, DORA signals and incident history to ownership, diagnosis and service or Terraform changes.

Human control: service owners validate diagnosis and remediation.

Agentic maturity

Six disciplines for scaling shared AI capabilities

The IMPACT framework turns a technology program into an operating model: a north star, repeatable behaviors, safe pilots, adoption and a platform that serves the process.

I

Inspirational vision

One north star: a governed, grounded engineering organization with measurable value and accountable outcomes.

M

Model the behaviors

Prompts, skills and evaluations become production artifacts; leaders and engineers work AI-first with disciplined review.

P

People & process first

Role-based agents mirror real engineering responsibilities. The ADLC lifecycle shapes the tooling, not the reverse.

A

Accelerated pilots

Fleet-level harnesses prove value across repositories before a winning agent or skill becomes a shared capability.

C

Change management

AI-native, in-repo handoff helps every team adopt shared agents and skills safely from day one.

T

Technology as enabler

Vendor-neutral gateways, evaluations, graph and memory serve the process, with open protocols supporting portability.

Govern continuously: risk classification, approval gates, audit trails, FinOps and standards alignment.
