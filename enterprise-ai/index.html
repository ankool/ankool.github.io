Lifecycle model

Product intent, delivery and telemetry

AI accelerates bounded work; product, engineering and service owners retain decision accountability.

AI PDLC

Idea to product direction

Ground ideas in strategy and customer context, then prepare requirements, Jira decomposition and design inputs.

Accountability: product and design owners approve value, scope and acceptance criteria.

AI SDLC

Story to reviewable change

Use repository standards and dependencies to plan, implement, test and review software changes.

Accountability: engineers approve architecture, test evidence, security and production risk.

TDLC

Telemetry to improvement

Connect traces, service levels, DORA signals and incident history to proposed service or infrastructure changes.

Accountability: service owners validate diagnosis and remediation.

Continuous governance: evaluation, approval controls, auditability, risk classification, FinOps, standards alignment and accountable human review.
