Agentic operating model

People, process and technology

Agentic maturity is more than tooling. A scalable model aligns engineering disciplines, the development lifecycle and a vendor-neutral technology foundation—with governance operating continuously across all three.

People & disciplines

Rings and disciplines give domain teams paved paths, non-engineering builders and role-based agents. Reskilling and clear ownership make adoption durable.

Process & governance

The ADLC lifecycle connects build, test, deploy, monitor and iterate. Evaluation, approval gates, audit trails and standards alignment remain continuous.

Technology as enabler

Vendor-neutral gateways, evaluation, graph and memory capabilities use open protocols such as MCP, A2A and ACP to serve the operating model.

Govern continuously: risk classification, approval gates, audit trails, FinOps and standards alignment.
